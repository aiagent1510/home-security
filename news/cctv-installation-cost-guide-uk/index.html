CCTV Installation Cost UK (2026 Price & Packages Guide)

Transparent UK CCTV installation costs for 2026. 2-camera (£495), 4-camera ColorVu (£695), 8-camera commercial packages. Zero monthly fees, local hard drive storage.

By Gary, ADI Communications · 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answers

How much does a typical 4-camera CCTV system cost to install in the UK?

A standard 4-camera 4K IP or ColorVu system costs between £695 and £995 fully supplied and fitted. This includes all external Cat6 cabling, discrete wall drilling, a 4-channel NVR with a 2TB surveillance-grade hard drive, power supplies, and full mobile smartphone app configuration.

Are there any monthly subscription fees with your CCTV systems?

No, absolutely none. Unlike consumer cameras such as Ring or Arlo that charge £40 to £100+ every year to view your recorded clips, our commercial systems record directly to your own local hard drive with £0 ongoing cloud fees.

How long does a CCTV installation take?

A domestic 2 to 4 camera installation takes between 4 and 7 hours to complete. All cables are neatly clipped along mortar lines or run through loft spaces, followed by camera alignment and app walkthrough.

Can I view my CCTV cameras on my smartphone when away from home?

Yes. We configure encrypted peer-to-peer remote access (Hik-Connect or DMSS) on your iPhone, Android, tablet, and smart TVs, allowing live streaming and instant playback from anywhere in the world.

What warranty do you provide on CCTV installations?

All our professional installations come with a full 1 to 3-year written hardware and labour guarantee, backed by over 25 years of local trade experience.
