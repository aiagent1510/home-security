UK Domestic CCTV Laws: ICO Guidelines & Neighbour Privacy

Complete legal guide to home CCTV in the UK. ICO guidelines, neighbour privacy masking, audio recording legality, warning signs, and avoiding GDPR harassment claims.

By Gary, ADI Communications · 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answers

Is it illegal for my neighbour's CCTV camera to point at my garden in the UK?

It is not strictly illegal to install cameras, but if a camera captures your private property or garden, your neighbour must comply with the Data Protection Act 2018. If they fail to provide a valid reason or refuse to use digital privacy masking, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) or take civil court action for breach of privacy.

Do I need to put up CCTV warning signs at my house?

If your cameras only capture within your private boundary (your driveway, front garden, and walls), you do not legally need signs. However, if your camera's field of view covers any part of a public pavement, shared driveway, or road, the ICO states you must display clear, visible warning signage.

Can I record sound/audio on my outdoor security cameras?

Audio recording is considered far more intrusive than video by UK courts. The ICO guidance explicitly warns against continuous audio recording in residential settings because it captures private conversations. Audio should be disabled on external cameras unless manually activated during two-way talk.

Can a neighbour demand to see footage recorded of them?

Yes. Under UK GDPR, individuals have the right to make a Subject Access Request (SAR) to see any footage in which they are identifiable. You have one calendar month to provide the footage, and you must redact or blur any third parties.

How does digital privacy masking work on professional CCTV?

In the NVR or camera software, an engineer draws black or blurred privacy blocks over specific areas of the screen (such as a neighbour's bathroom window or garden). The camera hardware blacks out those pixels permanently before recording, ensuring total privacy.
